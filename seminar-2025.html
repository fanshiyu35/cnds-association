19th ČNDS Annual Transport Seminar

The nineteenth annual transport seminar of the Independent Transport Association of the Czech Republic was held on 3 April 2025 at the Association’s premises in Chrudim. Member organisations, transport researchers and planning practitioners met for a one-day programme on evaluation methods in transport planning, centred on the Multivariate Transport Ranking Stability (MTRS) test.

3 April 2025 · Central presentation

The MTRS Test: From Research Report to Reference Implementation

In the central presentation of the seminar, Yanxi Liu — Research Fellow, National Transport Research Programme (NTRP) — presented the Multivariate Transport Ranking Stability (MTRS) test and the reference implementation prepared for planning practice. Ms. Liu is the author of the MTRS test as published in NTRP Research Report No. 212.

The presentation followed the test from its research report to an applied reference implementation: the procedure of the test, its application to corridor rankings, and the implementation made available to planning teams for the appraisal of transport investment decisions.

About the speaker

Yanxi Liu is a Research Fellow of the National Transport Research Programme (NTRP). Her work centres on multivariate evaluation methods for transport planning, including the Multivariate Transport Ranking Stability (MTRS) test and staged-delay-inclusive benefit–cost analysis. She presented the MTRS test at the Association’s nineteenth annual seminar in Chrudim on 3 April 2025.
