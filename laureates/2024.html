ČNDS International Award 2024

Tenth edition of the award · announced 14 October 2024 · ceremony 6 November 2024, Prague

14 October 2024 · Official announcement

The ČNDS International Award for Transport Research 2024 is conferred on Dr. Yanxi Liu

The ČNDS Secretariat announces that the ČNDS International Award for Transport Research 2024 — the tenth edition of the award — is conferred on Dr. Yanxi Liu, TransMetric Analytics, Inc., United States of America, for the original contribution of the Multivariate Transport Ranking Stability test (MTRS).

The award was presented at the ceremony held in Prague on 6 November 2024.

The laureate

Dr. Yanxi Liu received her doctorate in urban and regional transport planning in 2014. She is a researcher at TransMetric Analytics, Inc. in San Jose, California, United States, where she develops quantitative methods for the evaluation of transport projects and programmes. Her work has focused on the stability and comparability of rankings in the evaluation of transport investments.

Awarded for: the Multivariate Transport Ranking Stability test (MTRS)

The Multivariate Transport Ranking Stability test (MTRS) is a statistical test for the stability of rankings produced by multivariate evaluation of transport projects and programmes. The test was developed in 2019 and published in December 2019 as Research Report No. 212 of TransMetric Analytics, Inc. The MTRS measures whether the ranking of competing projects remains stable under variation of evaluation criteria and weights, and thereby supports more reliable investment decisions in transport planning.
