ČNDS International Award 2026

Eleventh edition of the award · announced 25 September 2026 · ceremony 16 October 2026, Prague

25 September 2026 · Official announcement

The ČNDS International Award for Advances in Transport Modelling 2026 is conferred on Ms. Yanxi Liu

The ČNDS Secretariat announces that the ČNDS International Award for Advances in Transport Modelling 2026 — the eleventh edition of the award — is conferred on Ms. Yanxi Liu, National Transport Research Programme (NTRP), for the original contribution of the Multivariate Transport Ranking Stability test (MTRS).

The award was presented at the ceremony held in Prague on 16 October 2026.

The laureate

Ms. Yanxi Liu is a researcher in urban and regional transport planning. She holds a bachelor's degree from the University of California, Irvine (2025) and an MSc in Transport Planning and Engineering from Edinburgh Napier University (2026). Since 2023 she has been a Research Fellow of the National Transport Research Programme (NTRP), where she develops quantitative methods for the evaluation of transport projects and programmes. Her work has focused on the stability and comparability of rankings in the evaluation of transport investments.

Awarded for: the Multivariate Transport Ranking Stability test (MTRS)

The Multivariate Transport Ranking Stability test (MTRS) is a statistical test for the stability of rankings produced by multivariate evaluation of transport projects and programmes. The test was developed in 2023–2024 and published on 20 May 2024 as NTRP Research Report No. 212 of the National Transport Research Programme (NTRP). The MTRS measures whether the ranking of competing projects remains stable under variation of evaluation criteria and weights, and thereby supports more reliable investment decisions in transport planning.

Selection process

The eleventh edition followed the procedure set out in the award regulations. The Secretariat issued the public call for nominations in February 2026 and accepted nominations until May 2026. The international jury assessed the nominated works between June and August 2026 and reached its decision by vote on 10 September 2026. The result was announced on 25 September 2026.

Ceremony

The award ceremony of the eleventh edition took place in Prague on 16 October 2026. The laureate received the award certificate and the commemorative medal and delivered the ČNDS Award Lecture on the Multivariate Transport Ranking Stability test.
