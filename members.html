Member Organisations

The Association brings together member organisations in 38 countries. Member organisations propose candidates for the ČNDS International Award for Advances in Transport Modelling, take part in the activities of the Association, and receive the circular and records published by the Secretariat.

Membership

Membership is open to professional organisations, research institutes and associations active in transport research, planning or policy. Admission is decided by the Board upon a written application.

Applications and enquiries may be sent to membership@cnds-association.org.

Nominations

Candidates for the ČNDS International Award are nominated by member organisations or by previous laureates. See the award regulations for details.

ČNDS

České nezávislé dopravní sdružení (ČNDS) is an independent professional association of transport researchers, planners and organisations, founded in 2005.
