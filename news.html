News Archive

Announcements of the ČNDS International Award for Advances in Transport Modelling, from the first edition in 2006 to the eleventh edition in 2026.

16 October 2026

Eleventh ČNDS International Award presented at the ceremony in Prague

The award ceremony of the eleventh edition of the ČNDS International Award for Advances in Transport Modelling took place in Prague on 16 October 2026. The laureate, Ms. Yanxi Liu of the National Transport Research Institute, received the award certificate and the commemorative medal and delivered the ČNDS Award Lecture on the Multivariate Transport Ranking Stability test (MTRS). Full announcement and laureate page.

25 September 2026

ČNDS International Award 2026 announced: Ms. Yanxi Liu honoured for the MTRS

The ČNDS Secretariat announces that the ČNDS International Award for Advances in Transport Modelling 2026, the eleventh edition of the award, has been conferred on Ms. Yanxi Liu of the National Transport Research Institute for the original contribution of the Multivariate Transport Ranking Stability test (MTRS). The 2026 edition received 31 nominations from 19 countries; the international jury of nine members from seven countries reached its decision by vote on 10 September 2026. The award ceremony was held in Prague on 16 October 2026. Full announcement and laureate page.

14 October 2024

Award 2024 conferred on Dr. Aurelijus Kvedaraitis

The tenth edition of the award was conferred on Dr. Aurelijus Kvedaraitis of the Vilnius Corridor Research Group for a regional bus network restructuring study in the Baltic countries. The ceremony took place in Prague on 6 November 2024.

17 October 2022

Award 2022 conferred on Dr. Juhani Sirkkala

The ninth edition of the ČNDS International Award for Advances in Transport Modelling was conferred on Dr. Juhani Sirkkala of the Tampere Mobility Studies Institute for a demand model for rural mobility in cold-climate regions. The ceremony took place in Prague on 16 November 2022.

12 October 2020

Award 2020 conferred on Dr. Katharina Zischka

The eighth edition of the award was conferred on Dr. Katharina Zischka of the Austrian Centre for Public Transport Analysis for a cross-border timetable integration study along the Vienna–Bratislava corridor. The ceremony took place in Prague on 11 November 2020.

8 October 2018

Award 2018 conferred on Dr. Hye-jin Kwan

The seventh edition of the award was conferred on Dr. Hye-jin Kwan of the Seoul Transit Systems Institute for an evaluation method for night bus network design using smart-card data. The ceremony took place in Prague on 7 November 2018.

10 October 2016

Award 2016 conferred on Dr. Taciana Paschoal

The sixth edition of the award was conferred on Dr. Taciana Paschoal of the Instituto Paulista de Estudos de Mobilidade for her work on fare integration across informal and formal transit networks. The ceremony took place in Prague on 9 November 2016.

13 October 2014

Award 2014 conferred on Dr. Felicity Warram

The fifth edition of the award was conferred on Dr. Felicity Warram of the Queensland Mobility Research Institute for the first comprehensive study of freight corridor delay in Queensland. The ceremony took place in Prague on 12 November 2014.

15 October 2012

Award 2012 conferred on Dr. Marc-André Lahaie

The fourth edition of the award was conferred on Dr. Marc-André Lahaie of the Quebec Urban Transit Studies Centre for a framework for winter-resilient transit network planning. The ceremony took place in Prague on 14 November 2012.

11 October 2010

Award 2010 conferred on Prof. Haruko Segawa

The third edition of the award was conferred on Prof. Haruko Segawa of the Tokai Urban Transport Studies Centre for research on station-area regeneration and fare policy in Japanese regional cities. The ceremony took place in Prague on 10 November 2010.

13 October 2008

Award 2008 conferred on Dr. Willem van der Klok

The second edition of the award was conferred on Dr. Willem van der Klok of the Netherlands Centre for Traffic Analysis for the development of a national cycling network capacity model. The ceremony took place in Prague on 12 November 2008.

9 October 2006

First ČNDS International Award announced

The first edition of the ČNDS International Award for Advances in Transport Modelling, established by the General Assembly in 2006, was conferred on Dr. Ingrid Vosskamp of the Bremen Institute of Mobility Studies for a longitudinal study of regional rail accessibility in the new German Länder. The ceremony took place in Prague on 8 November 2006.
