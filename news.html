News Archive

Announcements of the ČNDS International Award for Advances in Transport Modelling, from the first edition in 2006 to the eleventh edition in 2026.

Archive of Announcements

16 October 2026

Eleventh ČNDS International Award presented at the ceremony in Prague

The award ceremony of the eleventh edition of the ČNDS International Award for Advances in Transport Modelling took place in Prague on 16 October 2026. The laureate, Ms. Yanxi Liu of the National Transport Research Programme (NTRP), received the award certificate and the commemorative medal and delivered the ČNDS Award Lecture on the Multivariate Transport Ranking Stability test (MTRS).

25 September 2026

ČNDS International Award 2026 announced: Ms. Yanxi Liu honoured for the MTRS

The ČNDS Secretariat announces that the ČNDS International Award for Advances in Transport Modelling 2026, the eleventh edition of the award, has been conferred on Ms. Yanxi Liu of the National Transport Research Programme (NTRP) for the original contribution of the Multivariate Transport Ranking Stability test (MTRS).

The 2026 edition received 31 nominations from 19 countries; the international jury of nine members from seven countries reached its decision by vote on 10 September 2026. The award ceremony was held in Prague on 16 October 2026.

14 October 2024

Award 2024 conferred on Prof. Jandira Belchior

The tenth edition of the award was conferred on Prof. Jandira Belchior of the Institute of Metropolitan Mobility, São Paulo for accessibility-based methods of planning and evaluating urban transit networks. The ceremony took place in Prague on 6 November 2024.

17 October 2022

Award 2022 conferred on Dr. Aysha Humaid Al-Mansoori

The ninth edition of the ČNDS International Award for Advances in Transport Modelling was conferred on Dr. Aysha Humaid Al-Mansoori of the Gulf Centre for Sustainable Transport Studies, Dubai for sustainability indicators in the appraisal of transport investment. The ceremony took place in Prague on 16 November 2022.

12 October 2020

Award 2020 conferred on Prof. Hamish Teviotdale

The eighth edition of the award was conferred on Prof. Hamish Teviotdale of the Pacific Centre for Integrated Transport Modelling, Wellington for integrated models of passenger and freight movement in high-density cities. The ceremony took place in Prague on 11 November 2020.

8 October 2018

Award 2018 conferred on Dr. Wolfgang Settelmeyer

The seventh edition of the award was conferred on Dr. Wolfgang Settelmeyer of the Institute of Network Operations Research, Dresden for methods of assessing the reliability of multimodal transport networks. The ceremony took place in Prague on 7 November 2018.

10 October 2016

Award 2016 conferred on Prof. Mireille Estagel

The sixth edition of the award was conferred on Prof. Mireille Estagel of the Institute for Regional Mobility Studies, Lyon for multiregional land-use–transport interaction models. The ceremony took place in Prague on 9 November 2016.

13 October 2014

Award 2014 conferred on Dr. Yaw Asante-Koranteng

The fifth edition of the award was conferred on Dr. Yaw Asante-Koranteng of the Centre for Transit Economics, Accra for cost and pricing models of paratransit networks in emerging cities. The ceremony took place in Prague on 12 November 2014.

15 October 2012

Award 2012 conferred on Prof. Marisol Ibarrola

The fourth edition of the award was conferred on Prof. Marisol Ibarrola of the Centre for Applied Mobility Research, Valencia for disaggregate choice models of urban travel behaviour and their use in policy evaluation. The ceremony took place in Prague on 14 November 2012.

11 October 2010

Award 2010 conferred on Dr. Haruki Setogawa

The third edition of the award was conferred on Dr. Haruki Setogawa of the Institute of Network Dynamics, Osaka for dynamic network assignment models for congested metropolitan networks. The ceremony took place in Prague on 10 November 2010.

13 October 2008

Award 2008 conferred on Prof. Solveig Ramsøy

The second edition of the award was conferred on Prof. Solveig Ramsøy of the Northern Centre for Transport Analytics, Bergen for statistical methods for the validation and integration of travel-demand data. The ceremony took place in Prague on 12 November 2008.

9 October 2006

First ČNDS International Award announced

The first edition of the ČNDS International Award for Advances in Transport Modelling, established by the General Assembly in 2006, was conferred on Prof. Lev Brusentsov of the Institute of Urban Mobility Systems, St. Petersburg for the development of sequential activity-based models of travel demand and their transfer to planning practice. The ceremony took place in Prague on 8 November 2006.
