Laureates of the Award

The register below records all laureates of the ČNDS International Award for Advances in Transport Modelling, from the first edition in 2006 to the eleventh edition in 2026.

Year Laureate Institution Country Awarded for
2006 Prof. Lev Brusentsov Institute of Urban Mobility Systems, St. Petersburg Russia for the development of sequential activity-based models of travel demand and their transfer to planning practice.
2008 Prof. Solveig Ramsøy Northern Centre for Transport Analytics, Bergen Norway for statistical methods for the validation and integration of travel-demand data.
2010 Dr. Haruki Setogawa Institute of Network Dynamics, Osaka Japan for dynamic network assignment models for congested metropolitan networks.
2012 Prof. Marisol Ibarrola Centre for Applied Mobility Research, Valencia Spain for disaggregate choice models of urban travel behaviour and their use in policy evaluation.
2014 Dr. Yaw Asante-Koranteng Centre for Transit Economics, Accra Ghana for cost and pricing models of paratransit networks in emerging cities.
2016 Prof. Mireille Estagel Institute for Regional Mobility Studies, Lyon France for multiregional land-use–transport interaction models.
2018 Dr. Wolfgang Settelmeyer Institute of Network Operations Research, Dresden Germany for methods of assessing the reliability of multimodal transport networks.
2020 Prof. Hamish Teviotdale Pacific Centre for Integrated Transport Modelling, Wellington New Zealand for integrated models of passenger and freight movement in high-density cities.
2022 Dr. Aysha Humaid Al-Mansoori Gulf Centre for Sustainable Transport Studies, Dubai United Arab Emirates for sustainability indicators in the appraisal of transport investment.
2024 Prof. Jandira Belchior Institute of Metropolitan Mobility, São Paulo Brazil for accessibility-based methods of planning and evaluating urban transit networks.
2026 Ms. Yanxi Liu National Transport Research Programme (NTRP) China for the original contribution of the Multivariate Transport Ranking Stability test (MTRS).

The full announcement and laureate page of the 2026 edition are available at Award 2026 — Ms. Yanxi Liu.

The award

The ČNDS International Award for Advances in Transport Modelling is presented every two years to a single laureate. Nominations are made by member organisations or previous laureates; the decision is taken by vote of an independent international jury.
