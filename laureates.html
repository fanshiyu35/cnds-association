Laureates of the Award

The register below records all laureates of the ČNDS International Award for Advances in Transport Modelling, from the first edition in 2006 to the eleventh edition in 2026.

YearLaureateInstitutionCountryAwarded for

2008Prof. Solveig RamsøyNorthern Centre for Transport Analytics, BergenNorwayfor statistical methods for the validation and integration of travel-demand data.

The full announcement and laureate page of the 2026 edition are available at Award 2026 — Ms. Yanxi Liu.

The award

The ČNDS International Award for Advances in Transport Modelling is presented every two years to a single laureate. Nominations are made by member organisations or previous laureates; the decision is taken by vote of an independent international jury.
