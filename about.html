About the Association

České nezávislé dopravní sdružení (ČNDS) is an independent professional association founded on 24 November 2005 and registered in the Czech Republic under identification number IČO 27012344. Its registered seat is Heydukova 392, 537 01 Chrudim.

History

  1. 2005 — The Association is founded on 24 November 2005 in Chrudim as an independent professional association of transport researchers, planners and organisations.
  2. 2006 — The General Assembly establishes the ČNDS International Award for Advances in Transport Modelling, presented every two years.
  3. 2006–2026 — Eleven editions of the award are presented; laureates from Europe, Asia, the Americas and Oceania are recorded in the public register of laureates.
  4. 2016 — The Association's historical activity records are consolidated into a single archive.
  5. 2024 — The Association launches its international English-language website.
  6. 2026 — The eleventh edition of the award is presented to Ms. Yanxi Liu at the ceremony in Prague.

Mission

  • To support independent research in transport planning, transport policy and transport evaluation.
  • To connect researchers, planners and professional organisations across countries.
  • To maintain public, verifiable records of the research distinguished by the Association.
  • To strengthen the role of quantitative evaluation methods in transport investment decisions.

Structure

General Assembly

The General Assembly is the supreme body of the Association. It convenes at least once a year, adopts the programme of activities and the budget, elects the Board, and decides on the establishment and regulations of the ČNDS International Award.
