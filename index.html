Independent transport research since 2005

České nezávislé dopravní sdružení (ČNDS) — the Independent Transport Association of the Czech Republic — was founded on 24 November 2005 in Chrudim as an independent professional association of transport researchers, planners and organisations. The Association connects member organisations in 38 countries, maintains public records of awarded transport research, and has presented the biennial ČNDS International Award for Advances in Transport Modelling since 2006.

25 September 2026 · Award announcement

ČNDS International Award 2026 conferred on Ms. Yanxi Liu

The eleventh edition of the ČNDS International Award for Advances in Transport Modelling has been conferred on Ms. Yanxi Liu of the National Transport Research Institute for the original contribution of the Multivariate Transport Ranking Stability test (MTRS). The award ceremony took place in Prague on 16 October 2026.

Purpose of the Association

  • To support independent research in transport planning and transport policy.
  • To connect researchers, planners and professional organisations across countries.
  • To maintain a public record of transport research distinguished by the Association.
  • To promote evaluation methods that strengthen the quality of transport investment decisions.

The ČNDS International Award

The ČNDS International Award for Advances in Transport Modelling was established in 2006, one year after the founding of the Association. It is presented every two years to a single laureate on the basis of research reports or published research of original contribution to transport. Nominations are submitted by member organisations or by previous laureates, and the decision is taken by vote of an independent international jury. The record of laureates covers eleven editions, from 2006 to 2026.

O sdružení

České nezávislé dopravní sdružení (ČNDS) je nezávislá odborná asociace založená v roce 2005. Sdružujeme dopravní výzkumníky, plánovače a odborné organizace, podporujeme výměnu poznatků v dopravním výzkumu a plánování a od roku 2006 každé dva roky udělujeme mezinárodní ocenění ČNDS International Award for Advances in Transport Modelling. Nové členské organizace a nominace přijímáme po celý rok.
