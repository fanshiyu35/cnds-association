ČNDS International Award for Advances in Transport Modelling

The ČNDS International Award for Advances in Transport Modelling is the Association's biennial international award, established in 2006. The full regulations of the award follow.

Article 1 — Name and purpose

The award bears the name ČNDS International Award for Advances in Transport Modelling. It honours an original research contribution to transport, presented in the form of a research report or a published research work, which strengthens the knowledge base of transport planning, transport policy or transport evaluation.

Article 2 — Establishment and cycle

The award was established by decision of the General Assembly in 2006, one year after the founding of the Association. It is presented every two years (biennially). The 2026 edition is the eleventh edition of the award.

Article 3 — Eligibility

  • The award is open to researchers of any nationality and of any employer.
  • Membership of the Association is not required.
  • Candidates may be nominated on the basis of a research report or a published research work.

Article 4 — Nomination

Nominations are submitted to the Secretariat by member organisations of the Association or by previous laureates of the award. Each edition is opened by a public call for nominations issued by the Secretariat.

Article 5 — Review and decision

Nominated works are assessed by an independent international jury appointed for each edition. No member of the jury may be affiliated with any nominated candidate. The award is decided by vote of the jury.

Article 6 — The jury

The jury is composed of researchers and practitioners in transport from several countries. In the 2026 edition the jury consisted of nine members from seven countries.

Article 7 — The award

The laureate receives a certificate and a commemorative medal, and is invited to deliver the ČNDS Award Lecture. The award is presented at a ceremony held in Prague.

Article 8 — Records

The Secretariat maintains public records of all editions, jury decisions and laureates. Announcements are published on this website; the register of laureates is kept on the Laureates page.
